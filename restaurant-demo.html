About Trattoria Bianca

Since opening in 2010, Trattoria Bianca has been a destination for those seeking authentic Italian dining. Our chef trained in Tuscany and sources only the finest ingredients from trusted suppliers. Every dish is prepared with the care and attention to detail that characterizes true Italian cooking.
